Shopify Image Too Large? Compress Without Losing Quality (2026)

Why image size matters on Shopify

Page speed is a ranking factor for Google and a conversion factor for customers. A store with heavy product images loads slowly, pushes bounce rates up, and quietly kills sales. Shopify recommends keeping product images under 100 KB when possible — but most sellers upload 1–5 MB camera files straight from their phone.

Target file sizes

ElementRecommended size
Product grid thumbnail20–40 KB
Product detail image60–120 KB
Zoom / high-res image150–300 KB
Lifestyle / banner100–200 KB

Smaller files mean faster first paint and a smoother browsing experience on mobile, where most Shopify traffic now comes from.

How to compress without losing quality

  • Use lossy compression at 70–80% quality — visually identical, 3–5x smaller.
  • Convert JPG and PNG to WEBP for 25–35% extra savings.
  • Keep the original aspect ratio — never stretch product shots.
  • Batch-process your whole catalog in one go.

Common mistakes

  • Uploading unedited 4000×6000 px photos straight from a DSLR.
  • Saving as PNG "to be safe" — PNG is larger and rarely needed for photos.
  • Forgetting mobile: a 3 MB hero image costs seconds on 4G.
