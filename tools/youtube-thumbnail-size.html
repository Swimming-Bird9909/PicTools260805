About the YouTube Thumbnail Maker

YouTube thumbnails are the single most important factor for video click-through rate. The optimal size is 1280×720 pixels (16:9 aspect ratio), with a maximum file size of 2MB. Use bold text, contrasting colors, and clear faces to maximize CTR.
