The Complete Guide to Amazon Product Image Sizes (2026)

Why image size matters on Amazon

Amazon is the world's most competitive e-commerce marketplace. Your product images are the single most important factor in whether a shopper clicks your listing. Images that are too small, blurry, or non-compliant can destroy your conversion rate — even if your product is the best in its category.

This guide covers everything you need to know about Amazon product image requirements in 2026.

The 2000×2000 sweet spot

While Amazon's minimum requirement is 1000×1000 pixels (to enable the zoom feature), the recommended size is 2000×2000 pixels. This is the sweet spot for several reasons:

  • Zoom quality: At 2000px, the zoomed image stays sharp on retina displays and large monitors.
  • Mobile clarity: Mobile shoppers see crisp images even when zooming in.
  • Future-proof: As displays improve, 2000px images remain sharp longer than 1000px.

The 85% fill requirement

Amazon requires that your product fill at least 85% of the image frame. This means the product should be the visual focus, with minimal empty space around it. Use our Amazon Image Size Converter to automatically add appropriate padding to meet this requirement.

Background requirements

The main product image must have a pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255). This isn't just a recommendation — Amazon rejects listings that don't comply. Gallery images can have lifestyle backgrounds.

Best practices

  1. Always use 2000×2000 — for main images, regardless of your product's natural aspect ratio.
  2. Show the product from multiple angles. Amazon allows up to 7-9 images per listing.
  3. Include infographics showing size, materials, or key features.
  4. Use lifestyle photos in gallery slots 2-7.
  5. Compress to under 1MB for fast page load — but never sacrifice quality.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using low-resolution images (below 1000px)
  • Adding text, logos, or watermarks to the main image
  • Using colored backgrounds for the main image
  • Including multiple products in a single image
  • Showing the product too small in the frame
