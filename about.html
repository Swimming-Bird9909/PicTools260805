About ImageFitly

Building the simplest, most private image toolkit for the creator economy.

Our Mission

ImageFitly exists to make image optimization effortless for everyone. Whether you're a solo Amazon seller, a Shopify store owner, an Etsy crafter, or a YouTube creator — your time should be spent creating great products and content, not fighting with image sizes, compression tools, and Photoshop.

Why we built this

Most image tools on the web have one of three problems: they're expensive, they upload your images to a server (privacy risk), or they're overloaded with features. We built ImageFitly to be the opposite: free, private, and focused.

How it works

All image processing happens directly in your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. When you upload an image, it never leaves your device. The image is processed locally, and you download the result. This is why we can offer the service completely free — there are no expensive servers to maintain.

What we offer

  • 10+ free tools — Resize, compress, convert, and remove backgrounds
  • Platform presets — One-click sizes for Amazon, Shopify, Etsy, eBay, Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, Twitter, Facebook
  • AI background removal — Powered by the same models as paid tools, but free
  • 100% private — No uploads, no tracking pixels, no data collection
  • Mobile-friendly — Works perfectly on any device

Our principles

  • Privacy first. We will never upload your images to a server.
  • Free forever. No paywalls, no premium tiers, no watermarks.
  • User-focused. Every feature exists because users asked for it.
  • Open & honest. No dark patterns, no hidden fees.
