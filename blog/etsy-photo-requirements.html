Etsy Photo Requirements: Size, Dimensions & Best Practices (2026)

Etsy image specs at a glance

RequirementValue
Minimum size1000 × 1000 px (recommended for zoom)
Maximum file size10 MB per image
Accepted formatsJPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP
Recommended aspect ratio1:1 (square)
Number of photosUp to 10 per listing

Why 1000×1000 is the magic number

Etsy enables a zoom view when your image is at least 1000 px on the longest side. Square 1:1 images display cleanly in the grid without awkward cropping, and they look consistent across the marketplace.

Best practices for Etsy listings

  • Lead with your hero shot on a clean, white or neutral background.
  • Show the product in use in photos 2–4 to build trust.
  • Keep file sizes under 1 MB for fast loading on mobile.
  • Use all 10 slots — more photos correlate with more sales.
