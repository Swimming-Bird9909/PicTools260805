Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

1. Overview

ImageFitly ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our website and services.

2. The short version

Your images are not uploaded to any server. All image processing happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. We do not see, store, or have access to any images you process using our tools.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information we do NOT collect

  • Your images (processed entirely in your browser)
  • Your personal files or documents
  • Any content you upload to our tools

3.2 Information we do collect

  • Anonymous analytics: We use privacy-friendly analytics (Plausible or Umami) to count page views. No personal identifiers, no IP addresses, no cross-site tracking.
  • Cookies: We use only essential cookies (if any). No advertising cookies, no third-party tracking cookies.
  • Contact form data: If you contact us, we receive your email address and message contents, which we use solely to respond to you.

4. How we use information

We use anonymous analytics to understand which tools are popular and to improve our service. We do not sell, share, or rent any information to third parties — because we don't have any personal information to sell.

5. Third-party services

For the AI background removal feature, we load the AI model from a CDN (Content Delivery Network). The model is downloaded to your browser and runs locally. We may use the following services:

  • Cloudflare — CDN and hosting
  • Plausible Analytics — Privacy-friendly analytics

6. Children's privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to:

  • Access any personal data we hold about you (we hold none)
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing
  • Data portability

Since we do not collect personal data, most of these rights are automatically satisfied. For any data we do hold (e.g., email correspondence), you can contact us to exercise these rights.

8. Security

We use HTTPS encryption across the entire site. Since images never leave your device, the security of your content is in your own hands.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact us
