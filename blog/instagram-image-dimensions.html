Instagram Image Sizes 2026: Every Format Explained

Why Instagram image size matters

Instagram automatically crops images that don't match its supported aspect ratios. Posting an incorrectly sized image results in awkward cropping, lost detail, and lower engagement. This guide covers every Instagram image format in 2026.

The 4:5 portrait advantage

The 4:5 portrait (1080×1350) takes up more vertical space in the feed than the 1:1 square. Studies show it gets more engagement and visibility because users see more of your image as they scroll.

Stories: 9:16 vertical

Instagram Stories and Reels are full-screen vertical (9:16). The safe zone for text and important elements is in the center, away from the top status bar and bottom controls.

Profile photo

Your profile photo displays at 110×110 pixels on mobile and 180×180 on desktop. Upload at 320×320 or higher for best sharpness on all devices.

Tips for best results

  • Always upload at 1080px wide — Instagram compresses to ~1350px max, so larger originals stay sharp.
  • Use the 4:5 portrait for max engagement on feed posts.
  • Save Stories and Reels at 1080×1920 for full-screen quality.
  • Keep text and key elements in the center safe zone of Stories.
