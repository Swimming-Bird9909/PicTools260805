eBay Image Requirements 2026: Size, Guidelines & Tips

eBay image guidelines

GuidelineRecommendation
Longest side1600 px (for zoom)
Minimum500 px
Maximum file size12 MB (JPG up to 7 MB historically)
FormatsJPG, PNG, GIF, BMP, TIFF, WEBP
Free photosUp to 24 per listing

The 1600 px rule

eBay activates its picture zoom when the longest side is 1600 px or more. Zoom lets buyers inspect detail, which builds confidence and reduces returns. Square or 4:3 images both work well in search thumbnails.

What to avoid

  • Watermarks or text overlays — eBay discourages them.
  • Tiny sub-500 px images that look low-quality in search.
  • Oversized 12 MB files that slow the listing page.
