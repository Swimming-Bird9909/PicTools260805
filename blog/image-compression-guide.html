The Ultimate Image Compression Guide for Faster Websites (2026)

Images are usually the heaviest part of a page

On the average website, images account for more than half of total page weight. Compressing them is the single highest-impact change you can make for speed — ahead of minifying CSS or deferring scripts.

Pick the right format first

  • Photographs → WEBP (or JPG as fallback).
  • Logos, icons, graphics → PNG or SVG.
  • Simple animations → WEBP or GIF.

Quality settings that look identical

FormatSuggested quality
JPG70–85%
WEBP (lossy)75–90%
PNGlossless, but prefer WEBP if no transparency needed

A simple workflow

  • Resize to the largest display size you actually need.
  • Export as WEBP at 80% quality.
  • If WEBP is unsupported, fall back to JPG at 80%.
  • Lazy-load images below the fold.
