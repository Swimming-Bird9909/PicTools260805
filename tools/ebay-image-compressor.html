About this tool

eBay recommends listing images be at least 500 pixels on the longest side, with 1600×1600 pixels being the sweet spot. Keeping file sizes under 500KB ensures fast page loads and better search placement. Our compressor reduces file size while keeping your product photos crystal clear.
